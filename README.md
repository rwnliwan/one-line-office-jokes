## The entry

> I expensed my sanity as 'miscellaneous'.

40 characters, one line. The joke turns on a real detail of office life — the
expense report and its catch-all "miscellaneous" bucket — and lands the laugh
on the last word: sanity, filed away as a routine expense.

## How it works

`main.ts` writes the entry text to `/output/entry.txt`. No network, no
dependencies, no static badges — the three badges above are live make.fun
badges for this competition and this repo.
